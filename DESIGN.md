## Brand & Style

This design system translates the quiet grandeur and time-honored hospitality of a 150-year-old courtyard residence into a contemporary digital guest experience. It embodies an aesthetic philosophy defined by tactile calm, unhurried space, and architectural balance. The target audience comprises intentional travelers—cultural slow-travelers, writers, digital nomads requiring restorative tranquility, and couples seeking romantic authenticity over generic luxury.

The style unites **Minimalism** and **Tactile Architectural Framing**. Drawing from high-end architectural editorial layouts, the interface pairs generous, breathable canvas margins with structured organic cards, subtle border definition, and pill-shaped interactive anchors. The visual rhythm avoids aggressive sales patterns, replacing them with warm, grounding contrasts: earthy terracotta accents that signify welcoming warmth and human connection, deep forest greens reflecting foliage and age-old masonry, and an expansive linen-sand backdrop that feels like sunlit courtyards and textured lime-wash walls.

## Colors

The palette grounds the user in organic, sun-warmed materials. 

- **Primary (`#CC7A52` - Warm Terracotta):** Represents courtyard brick, terracotta tiles, and evening lanterns. Reserved strictly for primary action drivers, focused states, and key pricing callouts.
- **Secondary (`#2C4C3B` - Deep Forest Moss):** Evokes ancient banyan canopies and heritage shuttered woodwork. Used for prominent headers, brand signifiers, contextual badge fills, and structural frames.
- **Tertiary (`#8C6A48` - Sunbaked Earth):** A muted ochre-clay tone used for subtle metadata, icon accents, and tertiary dividers.
- **Neutral (`#232624` - Charcoal Slate):** Replaces pure digital black to preserve a soft, print-editorial contrast. All body typography, secondary headings, and form fields leverage this tone to eliminate eye strain.
- **Surface Sand (`#F4F1EA`):** The default root background color across all views. It rejects clinical white in favor of natural linen and lime-plaster warmth.
- **Surface Card (`#FAF8F5`):** A high-purity off-white used for elevated cards, booking containers, and focus widgets to float organically above the sand canvas without harsh drop shadows.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy pairs literary heritage with pristine contemporary legibility:

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the historical depth of the property through high-contrast serifs, classical proportions, and delicate terminal shapes. Used with modest weights (400 and 500) to maintain an unhurried, editorial character rather than heavy commercialism.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides geometric clarity, open letterforms, and warm humanist curves. This ensures effortless scanning of complex booking dates, amenities, room specifications, and long-stay Wi-Fi metrics across all viewport sizes.
- **Uppercase Overlines (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with extended tracking (`0.12em`) to introduce calm architectural section cues (e.g., "HERITAGE ESTATE • CIRCA 1874") without overpowering adjacent editorial titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a **12-column responsive fluid grid** with an intentional upper max-width of `1320px` to mirror luxury print magazines. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and outer canvas margins of `3rem`. Inter-section pacing adopts generous breathability with `space-3xl` (`6rem`) to cultivate calm.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins. Modules reflow into balanced 4-column groupings.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Booking controls consolidate into vertical progressive disclosure sheets.

The structural arrangement favors architectural asymmetrical balance: full-bleed image viewports juxtaposed with floating, inset container cards (`surface-card`) that overlap photography seams.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects intense artificial drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **ambient natural light**:

- **Canvas Foundation:** Layer 0 (`surface-sand`) represents the continuous wall and courtyard floor.
- **Raised Panels & Cards:** Layer 1 (`surface-card`) floats slightly above the sand surface using a hairline perimeter border (`1px solid #E3DCD2`) paired with an ultra-soft, warm ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(44, 76, 59, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35, 38, 36, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Booking Engine:** Layer 2 widgets utilize a warm frosted glass appearance (`rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by `#E3DCD2`.
- **Modals, Sheets, and Sticky Bars:** Layer 3 utilizes a focused ambient shadow (`0 20px 48px -8px rgba(35, 38, 36, 0.12)`) and sits prominently atop the viewport.

## Shapes

The design incorporates a hybrid shape language balancing architectural structure and biological softness:

- **Cards and Containers:** Use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to maintain structured framing without cold rigidity.
- **Buttons, Filter Chips, and Badges:** Leverage full pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for friendly tactile touchpoints that invite interaction.
- **Media Containers:** Image apertures use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) or specialized arched frames on featured storytelling panels, echoing courtyard colonnades and heritage veranda arches.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), Terracotta background (`#CC7A52`), white text (`#FAF8F5`), with internal padding `0.875rem 2rem`. On hover, transitions smoothly to a deep rust tone (`#B66640`) with an ambient lift.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pill-shaped, Forest Green outline (`1.5px solid #2C4C3B`), Forest Green text (`#2C4C3B`), transparent background. On hover, fills with Forest Green with `#FAF8F5` text.
- **Floating WhatsApp Anchor:** Fixed bottom-right circular badge (`56px x 56px`), `#25D366` surface with clean white icon, shielded by an ambient shadow to handle rapid guest inquiries.

### Integrated Booking Engine Widget
- A floating, unified pill or soft-cornered horizontal bar (`#FAF8F5`, hairline border `#E3DCD2`).
- Divided into internal segments: Check-in, Check-out, Guests, and "Check Availability" primary button.
- Clean divider lines in `#E3DCD2` separate date fields. Micro-labels are rendered in `label-caps` (`#8C6A48`), with active selections rendered in `body-md` Charcoal.

### Room & Suite Cards
- Architectural framing: Image occupies the top half with an internal `rounded-lg` radius, enclosed within a `#FAF8F5` container.
- Key highlights (e.g., "High-Speed Fiber Wi-Fi", "Courtyard View") presented as compact pill chips.
- Pricing displayed with baseline alignment: large Playfair numeral with charcoal sans-serif `/night` subtitle.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) metadata tags.
- Background: `#EDE8DE` with `#2C4C3B` text for amenities; deep `#2C4C3B` background with `#FAF8F5` text for exclusivity badges (e.g., "Heritage Suite").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Enclosed inputs with `#FAF8F5` fill, framed by `1px solid #E3DCD2`.
- Focus state switches border to `#CC7A52` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(204, 122, 82, 0.15)`).

### Guestbook & Review Cards
- Subtle off-white backgrounds with an arched quote icon accent in `#CC7A52`.
- Verified guest ratings highlighted with five stars in warm ochre, accompanied by guest location and length of stay tags.
